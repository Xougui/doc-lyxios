## What is `/config-user`?

The `/config-user` command allows each user to manage their personal preferences on the bot.

---

## Preferences Management

### Luckyblock Notifications

You can toggle ephemeral notifications sent when discovering a Luckyblock.

* **Enabled**: An ephemeral message appears whenever you find a Luckyblock.
* **Disabled**: Luckyblocks are added directly and silently to your inventory.
