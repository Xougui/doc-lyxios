If you encounter difficulties or have questions about using Lyxios, several resources are available to assist you.

## Help via the Bot

The fastest way to get information about features is to use the built-in command directly on your Discord server:

*   **/help**: Displays the complete list of available commands, categorized. You can also get details on a specific command to understand its options.
    * By using `/help`, you will see buttons like the <span class="discord-element discord-btn-secondary">Server support <ExternalLink size={14} style={{ marginLeft: '4px' }} /></span> button, which will redirect you to our official support server for personalized assistance.

## Support Server

Need personalized assistance? Our team and community are available to answer your questions, help with your configurations, or collect your bug reports and suggestions.
