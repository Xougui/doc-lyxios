Do you want to use Lyxios on your own server? Here is how to add it.

## Invitation Link

You can invite Lyxios to your server using the official invitation link below. Make sure you have the necessary permissions to add bots to your server:

<LinkButton
  href="https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1335228717403996160&scope=bot"
  variant="primary"
  icon="discord"
  target="_blank"
>
  Add to Server
</LinkButton>

## Via Discord Profile

You can also invite Lyxios by visiting its Discord profile and clicking the "Add to Server" button. Make sure you are logged into your Discord account and have the necessary permissions to add bots to your server.
