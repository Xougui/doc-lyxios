## Where to find the bump reminder module?

Direct command: `/config module:Rappels de Bump`

## What is it for?

Monitors Disboard `/bump` commands, sends a thank-you message, and schedules a reminder 2 hours later.

---

## Module Configuration

<Steps>
1. **Enable or Disable**: Use <span class="discord-element discord-btn-success">✔️ Enable</span> or <span class="discord-element discord-btn-danger">✖️ Disable</span>.
2. **Reminder message**: Click <span class="discord-element discord-btn-primary">Reminder Message</span>.
3. **Thank you message**: Click <span class="discord-element discord-btn-primary">Thank You Message</span>.
4. **Color**: Click <span class="discord-element discord-btn-primary">Color</span>.
5. **Role mention**: Dropdown <span class="discord-element discord-select">Select a role to mention... <ChevronDown size={14} style={{ marginLeft: '4px' }} /></span>.
6. **Remove mention**: Click <span class="discord-element discord-btn-secondary">Remove mention</span>.
</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Disboard Compatibility">
Works exclusively with Disboard.
</Aside>
