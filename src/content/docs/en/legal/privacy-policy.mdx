At Lyxios, transparency is our priority. This policy details the technical and personal data we collect to ensure the proper functioning of our services (Dashboard, Logs, Moderation), in strict compliance with Discord's terms of service and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Collected Information

To provide its services, Lyxios processes the following types of data:

### A. Identification Data (Discord)
* **Identifiers (IDs):** User ID, Server ID (Guild ID), Channel IDs, and Role IDs.
* **Public Information:** Username, avatar, discriminator, and account creation date (via the Discord API). This data is processed to identify the user, generate custom visual elements (Welcome/Goodbye), log their arrival in the server logs, and validate security checks (minimum account age for Captcha or Anti-Raid).
* **Server Data (Guilds):** When Lyxios is added to or removed from a server, we collect the server name, its ID, the number of human members, as well as the Discord ID and username of the server owner. This information is shared publicly on our support server (via a dedicated logs channel) for transparency and tracking bot growth.

### B. Configuration Data
*Data voluntarily provided by administrators to configure modules:*
* **Custom & Sticky Messages:** Content of Welcome, Goodbye, Bump Reminder, Anti-link messages, and Sticky Messages (persistent messages with last message ID tracking).
* **Custom Commands:** Custom trigger names, response text or embed contents, and creator Discord IDs.
* **Media:** URLs of images used for Welcome and Goodbye modules.
* **Moderation & Utility Settings:** Allowed domains lists (whitelist), ignored roles, logs configuration, Reaction Roles, Giveaways, Separators, Temporary Voices, Recruitment (application questions, allowed roles, log channels), Starboard (designated channel, star thresholds, and self-star settings), Member Counters (configured voice channels and naming formats), Staff Roster (organization categories and staff roles), Reports (moderation report intake channel), Server Blacklist, and Ghost Ping monitored channels.

### C. Technical and Security Data
* **Cloudflare Turnstile:** When logging into the Dashboard, we use Cloudflare Turnstile to verify that you are human. This may involve the collection of your IP address and browsing data by Cloudflare.
* **Anti-Scam, Anti-Raid, Anti-Spam, Anti-Bot and Surveillance System:** Lyxios analyzes messages, behaviors, links (URLs), and domain extensions (including sensitive extensions like `.zip`, `.mov`, `.download`) in real-time to intercept spam, phishing, scam, or malicious link sharing. An Anti-Raid module allows administrators to block suspicious new members based on account age. An Anti-Bot module prevents unauthorized third-party bot integrations. A Ghost Ping module detects deleted messages containing mentions to alert staff. Messages analyzed are not permanently stored for these features.
* **Verification (Captcha):** Temporary tracking of a user's verification state to prevent malicious bot access. Active captcha states are automatically purged.
* **Presence Cache (Status Autorole):** Detected custom statuses are kept in an in-memory cache with timestamps for a maximum of 24 hours (or immediately purged when the member leaves) to prevent prolonged storage.
* **System Backups (Internal):** Compressed copies of operational databases are periodically archived and securely transmitted to a private, restricted Discord channel reserved for Lyxios infrastructure administrators for disaster recovery purposes.

### D. Profile and Economy Data
* **Global Economy (Lyxios):** Coin balance, Luckyblocks inventory, unlocked fragments, date of last daily claim, and daily streak.
* **Server Local Economy:** Wallet (cash) and bank balances specific to each server, cooldowns (for work, daily income, or rob commands), and inventory of items or roles purchased from the server's custom shop.
* **Profile:** Unlocked images (Gallery), custom background image, blur level, and profile display preferences.

### E. Temporary Data
* **Reminders:** Content of the reminder message and scheduled date (automatically deleted once sent and purged per retention policies).
* **Love Calculator:** Compatibility percentage calculated between two users (temporarily stored to avoid re-calculation spam).
* **Giveaways:** List of participant user IDs for the duration of the draw.
* **Temporary Voices:** Owner user ID of the channel to manage permissions.
* **Recruitment (Applications):** Application metadata (User ID, Guild ID, status, and Discord message ID). Form responses are not stored in the Lyxios database; they are forwarded in real-time to the server's configured recruitment log channel.
* **Starboard:** Original message ID, Starboard post ID, author ID (`author_id`), and star count.
* **Reports:** Report metadata (reporter ID, reported target — member or message —, reason) forwarded to the designated moderation channel without unnecessary storage.
* **Weather Requests:** Temporary query of the city name or location transmitted to the external weather API to fulfill `/meteo` command requests, without logging or user association.

### F. Moderation History
*To allow community management and security:*
* **Warnings (Warns):** Reason for the sanction, moderator ID, and date. This data is linked to your user ID and the concerned server.
* **Server Blacklist:** Discord user ID barred from bot commands on the server, username, display name, reason for blacklisting, and moderator ID.

### G. Feature Tracking
* **Partnerships:** Storage of IDs (Message, User, Channel) for tracking advertisements, without content retention.
* **Confessions:** Only the total count of confessions is stored. The author's Discord ID is temporarily kept (maximum 7 days) solely for moderation purposes in case of abuse. The text content of confessions is never saved in the database.
* **Entraide (Anonymous Mutual Aid):** Storage of requester and helper IDs, temporary threads created, and links to their Discord avatars. This information is only used to relay messages anonymously and manage the voluntary identity reveal preferences of the participants.
* **Server Bump Tracking:** Discord user ID of members running bump commands (e.g., Disboard), cumulative bump counts, and last bump timestamp to maintain server bump leaderboards.

### H. Statistics and Performances
* **Vercel Web Analytics & Speed Insights:** Our main site uses Vercel Web Analytics (for anonymous traffic statistics) and Speed Insights (to analyze loading performance). Our documentation only uses Vercel Web Analytics. These tools collect non-identifying navigation data (visited pages, load times, country of origin) to help us improve user experience, without intrusive tracking cookies.
* **Bot Command Usage:** We record executed commands (user ID, command name, server ID, and timestamp). This data is solely used to compile global usage statistics (determining most popular features) and to assist in troubleshooting technical issues (debugging).

## Use of Information and Legal Bases (Art. 6 GDPR)

The collected data is strictly necessary for the operation of our modules. In accordance with **Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, processing is based on the following legal grounds:

*   **Performance of Service (Art. 6.1.b GDPR):**
    - **Welcome & Departures:** Sending custom images and messages when a member joins or leaves the server.
    - **Ticket & Report Systems:** Managing ticket workflows, staff routing, and moderation report delivery.
    - **Logs System:** Recording actions (message deletion, bans, voice activity) at the request of server administrators.
    - **Economy & Profile:** Saving progress, global and local balances, inventories, server shop items, and profile customization.
    - **Utilities & Engagement:** Managing personal reminders, temporary roles, Starboard, Sticky Messages, Member Counters, Staff Roster, and server bump tracking.
    - **Recruitment:** Managing application campaigns and assigning roles to accepted candidates.
    - **Entraide:** Connecting and relaying anonymous messages between help seekers and helpers.
*   **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6.1.f GDPR):**
    - **Security and Abuse Prevention:** Analyzing messages to block malicious links, intercepting scam/phishing, ghost ping detection, anti-bot controls, anti-raid protection, server blacklist enforcement, and automated Captcha verification to protect infrastructure integrity and community security.
    - **Maintenance & Debugging:** Diagnosing technical errors, automated disaster backups, and monitoring global service performance.

## Qualification of GDPR Roles (Art. 28 GDPR)

To clearly delimit legal responsibilities under the GDPR:

* **Lyxios as Data Controller:** Lyxios acts as a Data Controller for global user account management, global virtual economy, user profiles, global bot security systems (Anti-Scam, Anti-Raid), and Dashboard authentication.
* **Lyxios as Technical Data Processor:** For server-level management and logging features configured by Discord server administrators (specifically **Logs**, **Tickets** transcripts, **Local Server Economy**, **Starboard**, **Sticky Messages**, **Server Blacklist**, **Reports**, and **Recruitment** form responses forwarded to guild channels), the **Discord server administrator acts as the Data Controller**. Lyxios acts strictly as a **Technical Data Processor** executing instructions specified by server settings. Server administrators are responsible for informing their community members of the monitoring and logging tools active on their server.

## Data Retention and Purge Policy

* **Server Data:** Configuration data (Logs, Tickets, Anti-raid, Local Economy, Starboard, Sticky Messages, etc.) is retained as long as the bot is present on your server. If the bot is kicked/banned, this data is automatically deleted from our database. *(You can force immediate deletion via the `/reset-data` command.)*
* **User Data:** Data related to your global user account (Economy, Luckyblocks, Inventory, Profile) as well as your server-specific economy balances and inventories are retained until you request their deletion.
* **Automatic Purge and Retention:** Expired temporary records (due autorole removals, expired reminders) are automatically purged following a maximum retention window of 30 days.

## Your Rights & Right to Be Forgotten (GDPR)

In compliance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation EU 2016/679)** and Discord developer policies, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

* **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR):** Obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed and receive a copy thereof.
* **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR):** Request the correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
* **Right to Erasure / Right to Be Forgotten (Art. 17 GDPR):** Request the permanent and irreversible deletion of your personal data.
* **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR):** Request the temporary freeze of processing of your data under legally specified circumstances.
* **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR):** Receive your provided personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
* **Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR):** Object at any time, on legitimate grounds, to the processing of your personal data based on legitimate interest.

### Self-Service Instant Deletion via the Bot
You can permanently delete all your personal data stored by Lyxios (economy balances, inventories, profiles, warnings, reminders, participations) directly from Discord at any time without waiting for manual intervention:

<Steps>
1. Run the slash command `/rgpd` in any server where Lyxios is present.
2. Click the <span class="discord-element discord-btn-danger">🗑️ Confirm deletion</span> button in the ephemeral confirmation dialog.
3. All your records across all Lyxios databases will be immediately and irreversibly erased.
</Steps>
