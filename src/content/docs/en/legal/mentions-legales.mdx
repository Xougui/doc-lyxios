In accordance with Article 6, I, 5° of the French LCEN law, anyone identifying illegal content or unlawful behavior involving the Lyxios bot may submit a reasoned notice.

<Steps>
1. **Written Notice:** Send an email to [info@lyxios.fr](mailto:info@lyxios.fr) (with optional copy to [xougui.7@gmail.com](mailto:xougui.7@gmail.com)) with the subject `[LCEN Report]`.
2. **Required Details:** Include the date, timestamp, relevant Discord IDs (User ID, Server ID, Message ID), an accurate description of the contested facts, and legal grounds.
3. **Processing:** Valid and complete reports are investigated within 24 to 48 business hours.
